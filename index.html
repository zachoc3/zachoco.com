About

Before AECI, I secured networks for hundreds of financial institutions at Jack Henry, and I started my career as a software developer. That background shapes how I approach security: understanding how systems are built makes them easier to defend.

Day to day, that means SIEM and EDR detection engineering, MITRE ATT&CK coverage mapping, threat hunting, deploying and integrating new security tooling, and handling incident escalations.

Security isn't just controls and tools. It's trust, accountability, and helping teams feel confident in the systems they rely on. I try to contribute to that through mentorship, collaboration, and practical leadership.

Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my family, golfing, and cooking.
